About

The man behind the billboard.

Steward Rivers. The One Strong Man.

Steward's been hauling in the 209 for over a decade. In 2025 he put his own name — and his own face — on the business and opened One Strong Man's doors, backed by his family.

That face is on the billboard, on the trailer, and on every job. When you book, the person who shows up is the person you saw. No rotating crews, no subcontractors, no surprises.

Clear, honest pricing. No surprises, no games. That's the whole business model.
