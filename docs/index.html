Common Questions

Quick answers.

Do you really do same-day pickup?

Yes, when slots are open — and they often are. Text your photos in the morning and there's a good chance the junk is gone by dinner. Weekends included.

Do I need to move anything myself?

No. Loading is the job. Point at it from the couch if you want — garage, backyard, upstairs bedroom, wherever it sits.

What won't you take?

Hazardous materials — paint, chemicals, asbestos, that kind of thing. Almost everything else is fair game. Not sure? Text a photo and ask.

Where does the junk go?

Usable stuff gets donated when possible. The rest goes to proper disposal and recycling — not dumped somewhere in the county.
